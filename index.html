Who We Are

Born from a Calling,
Built on Shared Purpose

Founded by healthcare and community leaders, Compassion360 was built on a simple belief: everyone deserves care delivered with dignity, compassion, and respect.

Rooted in Montgomery County, Maryland, we combine clinical expertise with deep community connections to provide reliable, culturally responsive healthcare support. We created Compassion360 to help close critical gaps in care: connecting families, healthcare providers, and communities to trusted staffing, transportation, and home care services.

At Compassion360, our name reflects our mission: delivering compassionate, person-centered care at every step, for every person we serve.

What We Offer

Our Pillars of
Comprehensive Care

Every service is designed around one question: what does this person actually need?

Nursing Staff Referrals

We connect healthcare facilities and families with thoroughly vetted, licensed nursing professionals. Whether you need short-term coverage or ongoing staffing support, we match the right professional to your exact needs, quickly and reliably.

Non-Medical Home Care

Compassionate, in-home support that preserves independence and quality of life. From companionship and daily living assistance to personal care and light household tasks, our caregivers treat every client like family.

Our Community

Everyone Deserves to Be
Seen and Supported

Seniors & Older Adults

Aging with grace means having the right support. We help older adults maintain their routines, dignity, and independence in the comfort of their own homes, on their own terms.

Individuals with Disabilities

We believe access to care is a right, not a privilege. Our team is trained to provide sensitive, personalized support that honors the full capacity and personhood of every individual we serve.

Those in Transition from Hospital to Home

Whether recovering from surgery, illness, or a health event, the transition home can be the most vulnerable time. We provide steady, compassionate support during those critical early weeks of healing.

Retirement & Nursing Assisted Living

You can't pour from an empty cup. We support family caregivers, retirement homes, and nursing assisted living facilities with respite care, professional backup, and peace of mind because the people caring for others deserve care too.

Ready to find the right professional?

Whether you need immediate coverage or are planning ahead, we're here to help make the right match.
