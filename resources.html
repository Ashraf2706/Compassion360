About Compassion360 LLC
Compassion360 LLC is a healthcare staffing and home care company based in Montgomery County, Maryland. We connect individuals, families, and healthcare organizations with trusted nursing professionals and compassionate in-home care services that promote dignity, independence, and quality of life.
Our name reflects our commitment to delivering compassionate, person-centered care from every angle. We believe every person deserves to be treated with dignity, respect, and kindness throughout their healthcare journey.
Compassion360 proudly serves Montgomery County and surrounding communities in Maryland. Please contact us to learn more about our current service areas.
Our Services
We offer two primary services:
Nursing staffing services for healthcare facilities and families
In-home care referral services that connect clients with compassionate caregivers who support daily living and independence.
We match healthcare facilities and families with thoroughly screened, licensed nursing professionals. Whether you need temporary coverage, long-term staffing, or supplemental support, we work to provide qualified professionals quickly and reliably.
Our referral service connects clients with experienced caregivers who can provide companionship, personal care, assistance with daily activities, and light household support, helping individuals remain safe and comfortable at home.

Our Commitment
Compassion360 was founded by healthcare and community leaders who recognized the need for more compassionate, culturally responsive care. We combine clinical expertise with strong community partnerships to provide personalized services centered on the unique needs of every individual.

Getting Started
Simply contact our team to discuss your needs. We’ll learn about your situation, answer your questions, and help connect you with the right nursing professional or caregiver.

The cost of our services depends on each client's individual care needs and the number of hours or days of service requested. Every client receives a personalized assessment, after which we provide a clear daily or hourly rate based on the recommended level of care. You are free to choose the schedule that works best for you, whether you need care for a few hours, several days, or on an ongoing basis.
Compassion360 is currently completing the required enrollment and approval process with the Maryland Department of Health's Office of Health Care Quality (OHCQ) to provide services to clients covered by Medicaid.
