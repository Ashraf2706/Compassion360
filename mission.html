About Us / Mission & Vision

Guided by Mission,
Driven by Vision

We didn't start Compassion360 to build a business. We started it because we saw a problem that mattered and we believed we could be part of solving it, here in Maryland and eventually nationwide.

The Problem We're Solving

The Care Gap Is
Real and Urgent

Across communities like Montgomery County, there's a persistent gap between the care people need and the care they're able to access. That gap falls hardest on people who are older, lower-income, disabled, immigrant, or from non-English-speaking backgrounds.

It shows up as understaffed facilities, families struggling to find trustworthy home care, and patients discharged before they're ready. It shows up as people who could live independently with the right support instead losing that independence far too soon.

Compassion360 was built specifically to address that gap, as a sustainable, professional, and community-rooted organization that proves care done right can also be done reliably and affordably.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

To deliver compassionate, culturally sensitive, and affordable care services that uphold the dignity of every individual, connecting trusted professionals with those who need them most, right here in our community.

Our Vision

To become a nationally recognized model for inclusive, whole-person care expanding our footprint beyond Maryland to serve underserved communities across the country, proving that dignity and access are not luxuries.

"Care is not a transaction. It is a relationship, and we take that responsibility seriously."

— The Founders of Compassion360
The Road Ahead

Building From
Montgomery County Outward

Our national vision grows from our local commitment. We believe the model we're building here, done right, can be replicated in underserved communities across the country.

We start with depth, not breadth. Get it right in Maryland, build trust, build systems, build proof. Then scale what works.

01

Deep Roots in Montgomery County

Building our professional network, client base, and community partnerships in Montgomery County, Maryland establishing the operational model, quality standards, and community trust that will serve as our foundation.

02

Maryland & DC Metro Region

Extending our services across the broader DC metro area, Prince George's County, Northern Virginia, and Washington D.C. to reach the full diversity of this region's underserved populations.

03

A National Model for Inclusive Care

Bringing the Compassion360 model to other underserved communities across the United States, partnering with local organizations and professionals who share our values and commitment to dignified, accessible care.
